About ACT

Building Dignity Through Community Action

ACT believes lasting change begins when communities, especially women, lead it themselves. We walk alongside disadvantaged families as they build confidence, skills, and a voice of their own.

Our Vision

"To break blocking chains of social justice and to create a new social order."

Our Mission

"Equipping the downtrodden to live a dignified life through multi-pronged initiatives."

Who We Serve

Target Communities

ACT works primarily in Villupuram district, focusing our efforts on those most in need of support and advocacy:

  • Women and Children
  • Unorganised Workers
  • Dalit and Tribal Communities
  • Small & Marginal Farmers
  • Youth
  • People with Disabilities
How We Work

Our Approach & Principles

Building trust through frequent interaction with communities and leaders. Creating awareness through targeted information materials and campaigns.

Assessing community problems and identifying needs using Participatory Rural Appraisal (PRA) techniques to ensure local voices guide our interventions.

Building the capacity of target groups so that communities are equipped to manage and sustain their own development processes.
